Enterprise AI Company Handbook

Annual Leave Policy

Full-time employees are eligible for 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least 3 working days before the intended leave date.

Emergency leave can be requested without the normal advance notice requirement.

Leave requests are subject to manager approval.

Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to team requirements and manager approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during their assigned working hours.

Information Security Policy

Employees must not share company confidential information with external parties.

Company credentials must never be stored in plain text or shared with other employees.

Sensitive company data should only be accessed through authorized systems.
